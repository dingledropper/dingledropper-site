Sus Detector

Android · Privacy Audit · by dingledropper LABS
Available now

find out what your apps are really up to — on your phone, nowhere else.

Sus Detector is an Android privacy audit. It scans every installed app on your phone, reads each one's declared permissions, and tells you in plain English what each permission could be misused for. It runs a nine-check device-security audit over your network and hardware posture, sweeps for nearby trackers (AirTags, Tiles, unknown BLE), monitors your serving cell tower for IMSI-catcher patterns, and surfaces it all as one easy-to-read score.

Every computation happens on your phone. Sus Detector has no server, no analytics, no accounts, and no ads. Anything saved on your device is encrypted at rest with a key sealed in your phone's hardware-backed keystore.

What it does

🔍
App permission scanner
Inventories every installed app and reads its declared permissions. Each app gets a per-permission breakdown with a "sus" risk score and plain-English explanation of what each permission could be misused for.
🛡️
9-check device security audit
Audits Wi-Fi encryption, VPN status, DNS posture, Bluetooth visibility, USB-debug exposure, NFC, disk encryption, screen lock, and hotspot. Tells you what's off and how to fix each one.
📡
Nearby radar
Detects unknown Bluetooth Low Energy devices in your vicinity. Flags AirTags and Tile trackers that follow you across sessions. Scans Wi-Fi networks, NFC tags, and open LAN ports.
📶
Stingray watch
Monitors your serving cell tower in the background. Flags sudden tower swaps, unusually weak crypto, and other IMSI-catcher signatures — the patterns used to intercept calls and SMS.
👤
Security profiles
Five built-in profiles (Home, Shopping, Work, Travel, Lockdown) score your current device state against the profile's requirements so you can see at a glance what to fix for where you are.
🧹
One-tap nuke
A single button in Settings wipes every byte Sus Detector has stored on your device — trust overrides, tracker sightings, cell history, everything. Uninstall removes the rest.

How Sus Detector protects you

✓
Zero data leaves your phone. Sus Detector has no servers, no backend, no analytics, no crash reporters, no ad SDKs, no Firebase, no remote push tokens. The binary is auditable for the absence of network code touching user data.
✓
Encrypted at rest. Anything Sus Detector remembers between sessions — trust overrides, tracker sightings, cell-tower history — is stored using AES-256, with the key sealed in the Android hardware-backed keystore.
✓
No accounts, ever. Sus Detector has no sign-up, no login, no email collection, no anonymous-but-still-tracked device identifier. You install it, you use it, you uninstall it — that's the whole loop.
✓
No root required. Every feature works on a stock, unrooted Android phone. Sus Detector uses standard public Android APIs (PackageManager, NetworkStatsManager, TelephonyManager) — no exploits, no shell commands.
✓
Location stays on your phone. The app requests fine location because Android requires it to enable Bluetooth scanning and cell-tower reads on Android 10+. Sus Detector does not store, log, or transmit coordinates.
