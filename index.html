What we believe

Your identity, your habits, and your personal information should never be the price of using software. We build tools where you should never have to trust us with any of it — different products, different architectures, same principle.

Some of our software runs entirely on your device with no server at all. Some uses cryptography so we can verify things about you without ever learning what those things are. The common thread: your sensitive data stays on your side of the wire, and we build so you can verify that claim rather than taking our word for it.

SusDetector
Android · Privacy Audit

How SusDetector protects you
✓
Fully on-device. No server. No cloud. Everything the app computes stays on your phone and nowhere else.
✓
Zero data collection. Not "minimized", not "anonymized" — zero. The binary contains no code that transmits your data.
✓
No accounts, no analytics, no ads. No Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Sentry, no crash reporters, no third-party SDKs of any kind.
✓
One tap to erase everything. All data lives in local storage. Nuke button in Settings wipes it all. Uninstall removes the rest.

Sigil
Identity · Zero-Knowledge Proofs
Coming Soon

Prove things about yourself online — your age, your identity, your credentials — without handing over any of the underlying data. Zero-knowledge proofs mean the verifier learns "yes, this person is over 21" and nothing else. No name, no birthday, no document scan ever leaves your phone.

How Sigil protects you
✓
Your identity data never leaves your phone. Credentials are stored in your device's secure vault. Proofs are generated on-device — the server never sees the underlying data.
✓
Zero-knowledge verification. Verifiers learn exactly one thing (e.g. "over 21") and nothing else. Not your name, not your birthday, not your document number.
✓
Unlinkable across sites. Each verification produces a unique cryptographic nullifier scoped to that domain. No one can correlate your verifications across different services.
✓
Stateless relay. The Sigil server is a message router, not a database. It relays your proof to the blockchain and deletes the request immediately. No logs, no profiles, no history.
